## 7. Feature revenue participation

Feature-revenue participation is a separate agreement between the licensor/project,
the named feature contributor group, and the commercial payer or its designated
payment provider. It is not part of the customer's commercial license fee unless
the customer separately signs that arrangement.

For an official commercial product, the policy intent is to allocate 100% of the
revenue attributed to a specifically named feature to that feature's contributor
group. A low-cost virtual subledger may record the feature amount payable; it is
an accounting record only, not a bank escrow, trust, or legal custody account.
The signed payer arrangement must identify who owes the amount and, where
permitted, require the payer/payment provider to pay contributors directly. The
licensor/project is not presumed to receive the money or personally redistribute
it.

The group negotiates its internal shares. Before revenue accrues, the group must
accept a written negotiation deadline, neutral mediation process, and subsequent
independent adjudication or arbitration procedure. The project does not set
individual shares, impose a code-line or PR-weight formula, or decide disputes.
If shares are unresolved, the payer may leave the amount recorded as payable only
as its signed contract and applicable law allow; the project does not withdraw,
allocate, or distribute the disputed amount. Once the group provides a valid
written allocation and authorized payment instructions, the named payer/payment
provider pays contributors directly where lawful.

The separate agreement must complete the feature attribution and pool basis,
recordkeeping and statements, group membership, deadlines, mediator and
independent dispute forum, payment instructions, service fees and who bears them,
interest, refunds/chargebacks, tax reporting and withholding responsibilities,
failed payments, applicable law, and the treatment if no resolution is reached.
No bank, notary, regulated custodian, or payment provider is presumed available
or appointed by this template. The virtual ledger alone does not segregate funds
or establish a trust, escrow, or fiduciary relationship. Obtain qualified local
legal and tax advice before executing or operating the arrangement.
