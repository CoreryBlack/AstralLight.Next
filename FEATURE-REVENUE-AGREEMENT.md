# Feature Revenue Participation Agreement

**Status: non-executed discussion template; not legal, tax, escrow, or payment advice**

This template is for an official commercial product operated by the licensor or
project and a named feature whose attributable revenue is intended for its
contributor group. It creates no duty to collect, hold, or pay money unless all
required parties execute a complete agreement and the payment arrangement is
lawful and operationally available.

The policy intent is to allocate **100% of the revenue pool attributed to the
named feature** to that feature's contributor group. The group determines how
its pool is split among members. This arrangement does not apply to independent
forks, does not require any user to obtain a commercial license, and never
conditions or limits rights under `AGPL-3.0-or-later`.

## 7. Relationship to licenses and forks

This is a project-to-contributor revenue arrangement for the named official
product and feature only. It is independent of any commercial license granted to
a customer. Commercial-relicensing consent and participation in this revenue
arrangement are separate permissions and agreements.

Independent forks and other users exercising AGPL rights are outside this
agreement. No person must pay, register, report revenue, seek approval, join this
agreement, or share income to exercise rights granted by AGPL. Nothing here
changes the AGPL text or rights already granted under it.
