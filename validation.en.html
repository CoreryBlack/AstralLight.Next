02 Multi-Tenant Targets

What each of the five MT-E targets puts under load.

All five targets ran against real MySQL and passed. They cover interleaved isolation, authorization churn, concurrent publication, 512-tenant capacity, and mixed states.

MT-E1PASS

64-Tenant Interleaved Isolation

64 tenants · 8 readers / 8 writers · 1,024 reads

16 AllowActive, 32 DenyActive, and 16 DenySuspended; 16 cross-tenant probe pairs sampled in groups of 4 tenants all returned typed Pending. Covers tenant boundaries under interleaved reads and writes.

MT-E3PASS

8-Tenant Revoke / Recovery Churn

gen1 → gen5 · 4 readers · final exactly 1 grant

Five rounds of publish, revoke, recover, revoke again, and recover again; after each round converges, exactly 0 or 1 grants are asserted. Covers revoke/recovery convergence.

MT-E4PASS

16-Tenant Concurrent Publication

16 concurrent gen2 writers · 4 side readers

Each tenant concurrently advances from its own gen1 pointer to gen2; asserts Ready, exactly 1 grant for each card in its tenant, and provenance matching that tenant. Covers concurrent MySQL published-evidence updates.

MT-E5PASS

512-Tenant Capacity

seed 2.502s · publish 6.555s · read 2.002s

All 512 AllowActive cards were seeded, published individually, and read back with strict reads; the maximum single read was 5,366µs. The 512-tenant path passed.

MT-E6PASS

12-Tenant Mixed State

6 normal · 2 revoked · 2 suspended · 2 unpublished

Revoked/suspended fixtures are Ready with no grants; never-published returns NotReady + Pending. Covers fail-closed behavior across mixed states.
